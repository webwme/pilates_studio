About the studio

A higher level of Pilates

Polka is a place for people who want more than a workout. We blend the discipline of classical Pilates with contemporary, strength-focused programming.

Every class is capped at eight people, so your instructor can see and correct every movement. Whether you're brand new or years into your practice, you'll leave feeling taller, stronger and calmer.

FAQ

Good to know

I'm a complete beginner. Can I join?

Absolutely. Start with Mat Foundations or a beginner Reformer Flow; your instructor will guide you through every move.

What should I wear?

Comfortable, fitted activewear and grip socks (available to buy at reception).

How do I cancel or reschedule?

You can cancel free of charge up to 8 hours before class from your booking confirmation.

Do you offer private sessions?

Yes — one-to-one and duo sessions are available. Get in touch below and we'll match you with an instructor.
